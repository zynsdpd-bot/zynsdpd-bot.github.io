AI Tools for Beginners

This page introduces several widely used AI and creative services and the kinds of tasks they support. Some providers offer limited free access, while other features require a subscription. Availability and limits can change, so check each provider’s current pricing and terms.

Writing, research, and brainstorming

ChatGPT

Can help brainstorm, explain concepts, outline documents, and revise drafts. Verify factual claims with reliable sources and avoid entering confidential information unless you understand the service’s data controls.

Visual design

Canva

Provides templates and design tools for presentations, graphics, and social content. Check which assets and features are included in the free plan, and review licensing terms before publishing designs.

Video editing

CapCut

Offers video editing tools for trimming clips, adding captions, and assembling short-form videos. Features and export options may vary by region, device, and plan.

Notes and organization

Notion

Can organize notes, project plans, and team documentation. AI features and usage allowances may be subject to separate plan limits; consult the current plan details.

How to evaluate a tool

  • Identify the task you need to complete before choosing a service.
  • Compare free-plan limits, export options, privacy controls, and supported formats.
  • Test the output on a small, low-risk task and review it for accuracy.
  • Check ownership, licensing, and commercial-use terms for generated text, images, audio, and video.
  • Keep a human review step for anything public-facing or consequential.

A free plan does not mean every feature is free or unlimited. This page is an independent educational overview and is not an endorsement or guarantee of any provider’s services.
