About Practical AI Guides

Practical AI Guides is an educational website about using artificial intelligence tools in everyday creative and business workflows. Its topics include writing, video, design and automation.

Our approach

We aim to explain workflows in plain language, identify where human review is necessary and distinguish practical guidance from claims that cannot be verified. Tool capabilities, prices and terms change, so readers should confirm important details with the provider before relying on them.

Editorial principles

  • Prefer specific steps and useful examples over broad promises.
  • Do not present estimated earnings as guaranteed results.
  • Encourage readers to verify facts, licensing and platform policies.
  • Explain material limitations and risks alongside potential uses.

Content is general educational information and is not financial, legal or professional advice.
